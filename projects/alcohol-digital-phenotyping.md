# Daily Alcohol Use, Computational Markers, and Digital Phenotyping

<img src="/assets/img/project_coharu.png" alt="Smartphone-based monitoring study of alcohol use" class="project-full-img">

## Overview

This project examines whether daily fluctuations in computational decision markers and smartphone-based multimodal measures track real-world alcohol use.

Using 28-day monitoring data from adults with alcohol use disorder, the study integrates ecological momentary assessment, passive smartphone-derived measures, delay-discounting parameters, and clinical variables to model nighttime drinking.

## Research Question

Do day-level computational markers provide information about real-world alcohol-related behavior beyond self-report and passive smartphone-derived measures?

## Approach

- Processed and analyzed 28-day smartphone-based monitoring data
- Integrated ecological momentary assessment, passive smartphone-derived measures, computational markers, and clinical variables
- Examined same-day and next-day associations with nighttime drinking
- Evaluated whether computational markers add information beyond app-based self-reports and passive sensing data
