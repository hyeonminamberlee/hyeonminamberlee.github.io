# VR-Assisted Intervention for Social Anxiety

<img src="/assets/img/project_vr.png" alt="VR-assisted group CBT program for social anxiety" class="project-full-img">

## Overview

This project involved the development and administration of a 5-week VR-assisted group CBT program for college students with social anxiety.

The program integrated virtual-environment exposure exercises, clinical questionnaires, and wearable-derived heart-rate and heart-rate-variability measures.

## Research Question

Can a VR-assisted group CBT program support social anxiety reduction by combining exposure-based exercises with clinical and physiological assessment?

## Approach

- Helped design a 5-week VR-assisted group CBT program
- Assisted in administering VR-based exposure exercises
- Collected clinical questionnaire and wearable-derived physiological measures
- Analyzed behavioral data and prepared results for poster presentation

## Output

Poster presented at the World Congress of Cognitive and Behavioral Therapies.  
Received the Seoul National University President’s Award from the Undergraduate Research Support Program.
